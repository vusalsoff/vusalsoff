## About me

I'm **Vusal Suleymanov**, a Computer Engineering student at **Azerbaijan State Agricultural University (ADAU)**. I build software, experiment with artificial intelligence and embedded systems, and enjoy transforming early-stage concepts into usable prototypes.

My interests sit at the intersection of **AI, full-stack development, robotics, and entrepreneurship**. I also enjoy mentoring aspiring developers, collaborating with ambitious teams, and tracking down the bugs that others find hard to reproduce.

- 🏆 **1st place — Karabakh Hackathon 2026**, with **Team Code44** and the **e-PinAgro** project.
- 🔭 **Currently building:** RoboX and experimental AI-powered systems.
- 🌱 **Currently learning:** React Native, cross-platform mobile development, and .NET.
- 🤝 **Open to:** meaningful collaborations in AI, developer tools, robotics, agritech, and educational technology.
- 🐛 **Favorite challenge:** debugging complex issues and making systems more reliable.
